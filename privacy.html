Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Replace the placeholders below (business name, contact email) with your own details before launch.

1. What we collect

When you use VowCraft we process: the occasion, tone, names, and personal details you type in, in order to generate your speech; and standard payment information handled entirely by Stripe (we never see or store your card number).

2. How it's used

Your speech details are sent to OpenAI's API to generate the draft and are kept in server memory only long enough to let you preview, pay for, and download your speech (drafts are automatically deleted after 2 hours). We do not sell your information.

3. Third parties

We share data with two processors only: OpenAI (to generate the speech text) and Stripe (to process payment). Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

4. Data retention

Generated speech drafts are held in memory for up to 2 hours and are not permanently stored or backed up by VowCraft. Once you download or copy your speech, keep your own copy — we cannot retrieve it later.

5. Cookies

VowCraft does not use tracking cookies. Stripe Checkout may set its own cookies during the payment step, governed by Stripe's privacy policy.
